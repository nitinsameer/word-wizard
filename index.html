1. Overview

Welcome to OwlZee: Grammar Games for Kids, an English grammar learning app designed for children aged 3–10. OwlZee is developed and published by Nishaan Tech, an independent app studio based in India.

We take the privacy and safety of children extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information OwlZee collects (very little), what it does not collect, and how we protect your child's experience.

Short version: OwlZee does not collect, store, or share any personal information about your child. All game progress is stored locally on your device only.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

OwlZee is designed with a privacy-first, no-data approach. We explicitly do not collect:

  • ❌ Full name, email address, or phone number
  • ❌ Date of birth or age verification data
  • ❌ Device identifiers (IMEI, Advertising ID)
  • ❌ Location data (GPS or IP-based)
  • ❌ Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • ❌ Contacts or social connections
  • ❌ Payment or financial information
  • ❌ Browsing history or cross-app tracking
  • ❌ Voice recordings or audio
  • ❌ Any biometric data
No account required. OwlZee does not require users to create an account, log in, or register. There is no sign-up flow.
⚙️

5. How Data Is Used

The limited local data stored on your device is used solely to:

  • Display the child's nickname and personalise the experience
  • Save and restore game progress across sessions
  • Track stars, coins, XP, streaks, and badges earned
  • Show the Parent Dashboard with learning progress
  • Remember sound and haptic preferences
We never use data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose. There are no targeted ads in OwlZee.
💾

6. Data Storage & Retention

All game data is stored locally on the child's device using Android's secure local storage (Room Database / SharedPreferences).

  • Data is never uploaded to Nishaan Tech servers
  • Data is never synced to cloud services
  • Data is never backed up to Google Drive or any external service
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data
  • Parents can also use the "Reset Progress" option within the app
Data deletion: To delete all data, simply uninstall OwlZee from the device. All locally stored data is permanently removed.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

OwlZee is designed specifically for children aged 3–10 years. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not allow children to communicate with other users
  • We do not include social features, chats, or forums
  • We do not display behavioural or interest-based advertising
  • We do not link to external websites from within the app
  • Parental consent is sought before any optional data collection
OwlZee complies with Google Play's Families Policy and is designed to be safe for children without any parental supervision concerns.

The app features a Parent / Guardian Consent screen on first launch, requiring a parent to confirm consent before the child can use the app. This screen is designed to be completed by an adult.

👪

9. Parental Rights & Controls

As a parent or guardian, you have full control over your child's data and experience in OwlZee:

  • View Progress: Use the Parent Dashboard within the app (Settings → Parent Dashboard) to view your child's learning progress, accuracy, streaks, and badges
  • Manage Settings: Enable or disable sound effects, background music, and haptic feedback at any time
  • Withdraw Analytics Consent: Contact us at nitinsameer@gmail.com to withdraw optional analytics consent
  • Delete All Data: Uninstall the app from the device to permanently delete all locally stored data
  • Contact Us: Email us with any privacy concerns and we will respond within 5 business days
Parents can request data deletion or ask privacy questions by emailing nitinsameer@gmail.com. We will respond within 5 business days.
📈

10. Anonymous Analytics (Optional)

During first-time setup, parents are given the option to allow anonymous usage analytics. This is entirely voluntary and the app works fully without it.

If opted in, we may collect:

  • App crash reports (no personal data, just error logs)
  • General usage patterns (e.g. which worlds are played most)
  • Device type and Android version (for compatibility)
This data is completely anonymous. It cannot be used to identify any individual child or user. It is used only to improve app stability and content quality.

This analytics consent can be withdrawn at any time by emailing nitinsameer@gmail.com.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data within OwlZee:

  • All data is stored using Android's secure local storage mechanisms
  • The app does not transmit personal data over the internet
  • No user accounts means no password or credential risks
  • The app does not require internet connectivity to function
  • We regularly review and update the app for security vulnerabilities
Since OwlZee stores all data locally and collects no personal information, the attack surface for data breaches is essentially zero.
